Keys and rules

1 empty   2 half   3 full   W wrong product (toggle)   ← → previous / next   ↑ ↓ previous / next unlabelled   Backspace clear   A auto-advance   D download labels.json

  • Stock level = how full the area looks: empty (almost nothing), half, full. Judge what is visible in the straightened area.
  • Wrong product = the area shows a different product than the expected one (a different fruit, not just a different variety). Press W before the stock level, because the level key moves on to the next tile.
  • An empty area is normally compliant (nothing wrong is shown). Parts outside the frame are black: ignore them.
  • Labels are saved in this browser automatically. Download labels.json when you are done (annotations.json format).
