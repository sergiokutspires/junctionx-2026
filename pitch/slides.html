04 · How it works, with no labels

Rectify, look, name, then decide for the score

1 Perspective-warp every planogram area to a square crop. These are the real crops from the frame on the previous slide.

2 How full? stock

DINOv2 patches are clustered. Clusters found under every product are surface; clusters tied to a few fruits are product. CLIP names them. Fill = share of product patches, then empty / half / full.

3 Right product? compliance

CLIP zero-shot against the catalogue, at base-fruit level: banana vs orange vs apple. Unsure, hidden or empty counts as compliant: no false alarms.

4 Decide for the score

Wrong identification costs 2. Stock too low costs 2, too high costs 3. We pick the answer with the best expected score, so we lean low when unsure.

5 Boxes and counts detection

OWLv2, an open-vocabulary detector, names the products on the shelf. The app draws them behind Show detections and counts them per area: ≈ N visible units, an estimate.
