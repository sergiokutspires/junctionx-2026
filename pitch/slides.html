Appendix A4 · Method and answer key

What runs, and what we scored against

Pipeline (CPU only)

  • Each planogram area is warped to a square crop.
  • Stock: DINOv2-small patches split into surface and product; fill = product share. Calibration heads and a per-area memory fitted on the key.
  • Product: CLIP B/16 (bf16) zero-shot on catalogue names at base-fruit level.
  • Decision: the answer with the best expected score under your rules; when unsure it answers lower.
  • Optional: OWLv2 boxes and “unidades visíveis” estimates for staff.

Answer key: made by AI models, no human labelling

  • 181 frames, 14 cameras, 1,962 areas.
  • Round 1 (cameras 6215, 6371, 6373): three Claude Opus 5.5 labellers plus adjudication.
  • Then single Claude Sonnet 5.5 votes, plus two more votes per area (Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5): 3 votes per area (36 areas have 2).
  • 84% unanimous on the 11 re-voted cameras; 34 disputes adjudicated by Opus 5.5.
  • Gemini Flash cross-check on 192 areas: 100% agreement on wrong-product calls, 81% on stock level.

Rubric: the GUIDE text in tools/gemini_label.py. Files: data/labels/teacher_labels_v2.json, votes_v2.json, adjudications_v2.json.

Why “late” is an assumption

The only hidden file name we know is the brief’s example, 1751052241.jpg = 20:24 Lisbon, later than our last picture (20:16). The brief’s 320 is a worked scoring example, not a benchmark.

Spread

Camera by camera, late two-picture scores run from 0.50 to 2.00 per area (folds of 12–32 areas). Scored against the AI-made key; Sensei’s own answers decide. Day-0 thresholds were tuned once on this store.

Source: results/calibration_cv_real_v3_cal.json (methods heads and baseline; per_camera). Label-free = the unsupervised core of the submitted model; the fallback models/real_v1 scores 1.21 / 1.01 / 0.98 on the same folds. Always “compliant, full” scores 0.76 whole day, 0.46 late.
