## How to record it in 15 minutes (free, on this laptop)

1. **Screen + voice:** open the website in Edge (full screen, F11), press **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar) to start
   and stop recording; it records the window and the microphone. Record scenes 2 to 6 in one take, reading the
   lines; Inês and Sérgio sit side by side so both voices are picked up.
2. **Faces (scenes 1 and 7):** film them with a phone, landscape, at arm's length, in a quiet corner with light on
   your faces.
3. **Join:** open **Clipchamp** (free, built into Windows 11), drop in clip 1, the screen recording and clip 7, add
   the text "sergiokutspires.github.io/junctionx-2026" at the end, export 1080p.
4. **Upload:** YouTube, **Unlisted**, title "fresh. explained by NovaLotus (JunctionX Lisbon 2026)".

## Rules

- Use the public website only (pixelated pictures). Never record the sharp team area or the laptop app.
- If a line feels long, cut it rather than speeding up. Under 90 seconds is the goal.
- A mistake? Pause two seconds and repeat the sentence; cut the bad take in Clipchamp.
