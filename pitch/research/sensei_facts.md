Source tags used below:
- **[S]** Sensei's own website or press (their words; marketing, treat numbers as vendor-reported).
- **[P]** Independent press, award body or investor (third party).

## 1. What Sensei does

- Builds autonomous (checkout-free) stores using ceiling cameras, computer vision, AI, shelf weight sensors and on-prem servers; sells it as stores, pods, cabinets and "lanes" (express checkout inside existing stores). [S] https://sensei.tech/ (undated; footer (c) 2024, banner Shoptalk Europe 9-11 June 2026)
- Self-description (home page): "We build autonomous stores designed for humans, using computer vision and artificial intelligence, that generate priceless insights and automation for retail businesses." [S] https://sensei.tech/ (undated)
- Hardware stack named on their supermarket page: monofocal ceiling cameras "identify products and track people anonymously"; computational servers "process data on-prem"; shelf weight sensors "adjust to any kind of shelf and package"; proprietary AI software "translates in-store images into valuable insights". [S] https://sensei.tech/industries/supermarkets (undated)
- Shopper side: no app, no scanning, no entrance barrier; a virtual basket is built as the shopper moves, shown on a terminal at exit; pay by card, cash or app. [S] https://sensei.tech/story/continente-bom-dia (undated)
- Retailer side: "real-time shelf inventory visibility", planogram/layout tools, replenishment alerts, heatmaps, shrink reduction. [S] https://sensei.tech/ and https://sensei.tech/industries/supermarkets (undated)
- Privacy position: GDPR-compliant, "cameras don't collect or store biometric data points", no facial recognition. [S] https://sensei.tech/ (undated); also [P] Observador 2019-03-19 (founders: "our technology is not facial recognition") https://observador.pt/2019/03/19/estes-portugueses-querem-acabar-com-as-caixas-de-supermercado/
- Tech-agnostic: integrates with ERPs, loyalty, payments, checkouts; exposes "data APIs"; runs a partner programme to scale "Retail AI". [S] https://sensei.tech/partners (undated)
- Their 2026 self-description to EU consortium matchmaking: "a Portuguese deep-tech company building AI and computer vision solutions"; stack "combines real-time computer vision, sensor fusion and AI agents"; validated "across a live, revenue-generating network". [P-listing] https://www.b2match.com/e/cl4-digital2026-matchmaking/opportunities/UGFydGljaXBhdGlvbk9wcG9ydHVuaXR5OjI2MjAzNQ== (profile updated 2026-09-09)

## 4. The Leiria store (the dataset's store) and why it is notable

- Name/location: Continente Bom Dia Sao Romao, Leiria; operated by MC (Sonae MC); built with Sensei. Opened Friday 2025-01-10. [P] https://echoboomer.pt/continente-leiria-maior-loja-inteligente-do-mundo/ (2025-01-10); [S] https://www.sensei.tech/insights/sensei-and-mc-sonae-unveil-worlds-largest-autonomous-supermarket-in-leiria
- Size and range: 1,200 m2; Sensei's launch note says "over 11,000 SKUs"; Sensei's 2026 pages say "over 12,000"; press said "more than 10,000 items". [S] launch note above; [S] https://www.sensei.tech/insights/sensei-recognised-across-global-retail-innovation-awards (undated); [P] https://retail-optimiser.de/en/mc-sonae-opens-large-scanless-supermarket-with-sensei/ (2025-01-29)
- Hardware (as reported at launch): 1,676 cameras, 2,000 scales, 21 servers (vendor figures). [P] https://retail-optimiser.de/en/mc-sonae-opens-large-scanless-supermarket-with-sensei/ (2025-01-29). A visiting professor says "more than 1,000 cameras". [S+] https://sensei.tech/insights/prof-dr-stephan-ruschen-on-the-real-test-of-autonomous-retail (undated)
- Format: hybrid, 6 autonomous checkouts (smart + self) and 2 manned tills; 55-space car park. [P] https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/?p=569614 (2025-01-13)
- Investment: EUR 6M (largest Bom Dia investment of 2024, financed under the PRR recovery plan), of which EUR 1.5M for Sensei technology; 28 jobs created; 17th Continente store in the Leiria district. [P] https://www.forbespt.com/sonae-abre-primeira-loja-inteligente-em-leiria (2025-01-11); https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/?p=569614 (2025-01-13)
- Why notable (their framing): billed as "the largest autonomous supermarket in the world" (a claim; Rewe's Trigo store in Hamburg is also large and the press notes the "largest" label is contestable). [S] launch note; [P] https://retail-optimiser.de/en/mc-sonae-opens-large-scanless-supermarket-with-sensei/ (2025-01-29)
- Why notable (their framing 2): it was designed to prove autonomy works without the limits of other models, i.e. "excluding assisted counters, selling loose fruit and vegetables by weight, accommodating different types of shelving, or displaying products on pallets". [S] https://sensei.tech/story/continente-bom-dia (undated)
- Vendor-reported results: >70% of transactions fully autonomous; 22.7 min decrease in average shopping time; "Top 10" Bom Dia store by sales (another Sensei page says "top five"); 9 daily replenishment tasks automatically generated; more than 3,000 baskets a day. [S] https://sensei.tech/story/continente-bom-dia (undated) and https://sensei.tech/insights/toby-pickard-visits-continente-bom-dia-leiria-autonomous-retail-at-full-scale (undated)
- Independent signal on results: the EHI award entry says the store reports "higher customer satisfaction" with sales and traffic comparable to similar stores. [P] https://www.reta-europe.com/artificial-intelligence-sonae/ (undated)
- Awards tied to the store: EuroCommerce Digitalisation 2025 (Sensei's claim); EHI reta AI category 2026 (verified, EHI); RTIH AI and Physical Stores 2026 (verified, RTIH). [S] https://www.sensei.tech/insights/sensei-recognised-across-global-retail-innovation-awards ; [P] EHI and RTIH links in section 3.

## 6. Shelf monitoring, planogram, replenishment, shrink, waste

- Leiria case study: "Sensei's platform handled planogram compliance, automating replenishment alerts for staff, measured product interactions and product conversion rates and generated customer movement heatmaps". [S] https://sensei.tech/story/continente-bom-dia (undated)

[S] https://sensei.tech/insights/toby-pickard-visits-continente-bom-dia-leiria-autonomous-retail-at-full-scale (undated)

- Shrink: "reducing shrinkage" (About); "virtually eliminate theft" (supermarkets page). [S] https://sensei.tech/company ; https://sensei.tech/industries/supermarkets
- Food waste: no Sensei-published waste figure or waste feature found. [none] (searched 2026-10-11)
